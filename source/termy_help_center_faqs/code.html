4 TOPICS

Termy continuously aligns syllabus modules with the latest Department of Examinations (DOE) and National Institute of Education (NIE) syllabus circulars. When updates occur (such as expanded Python 3.10+ questions, database normalization depth, or updated CIDR subnetting queries), your lesson path reorganizes so you practice high-frequency past paper patterns first.

Your streak increments when you complete at least one past paper MCQ drill or logic simulation before 23:59 IST. A Streak Freeze (purchasable with 120 Bits in the Shop) shields your streak from resetting if you miss a revision day due to tuition classes or mock exams. A maximum of two freezes can be equipped concurrently.

Leaderboard cohorts run on a 7-day cycle concluding every Sunday at 20:00 IST. The top 10 candidates with the highest accumulated XP from MCQ accuracy and timed speed-drills are promoted to the next tier (Bronze up to Diamond League). Demotion zones trigger for candidates in the lowest 5 ranks of their respective 30-candidate pool.
